ACME Corporation — Travel and Expense Policy

Business travel must have a clear business purpose and manager approval before booking. Use the company's approved booking and payment channels where available. Choose reasonable transportation and lodging that meet the trip's business needs and applicable safety requirements.

Employees should keep itemized receipts and submit an expense report through the finance system within 30 days of the trip. Each item must include the date, business purpose, project or cost center, and required receipt. Personal purchases, duplicate claims, and expenses for unauthorized guests are not reimbursable.

Meals should be modest and consistent with local guidance. Client or partner entertainment requires prior approval when required by the applicable policy. Do not split expenses to avoid an approval limit. If a receipt is unavailable or an exception is needed, explain the circumstances in the report and ask the manager or Finance for review.

Finance may return incomplete reports for correction. Employees and approvers are responsible for submitting accurate information and protecting payment details.
