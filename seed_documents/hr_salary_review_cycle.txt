ACME Corporation — HR Salary Review Cycle

Purpose
This guide describes the annual process HR uses to coordinate compensation reviews. Individual employee compensation decisions are confidential and should only be discussed with the employee, their authorized manager, and HR staff with a business need to know.

Annual schedule
1. HR publishes the review calendar and confirms the eligible employee population.
2. Managers submit role scope, performance context, and a written compensation recommendation by the published deadline.
3. HR checks recommendations for internal consistency, approved budgets, and applicable pay-equity requirements.
4. Department leaders complete calibration with HR. Recommendations remain provisional until approved through the company approval process.
5. Managers communicate approved decisions privately after HR confirms that the cycle is complete.

Review principles
Consider role responsibilities, sustained impact, skills, market information, and internal equity. Do not promise a raise before approval. Do not use protected personal characteristics as a factor. Record the business rationale in the approved HR system and retain it according to the records schedule.

Questions about eligibility, exceptions, or corrections should be sent to the HR compensation team. Do not send employee compensation details through public chat channels or attach them to general project documents.
