ACME Corporation — HR Recruitment and Interview Guide

Hiring process
1. The hiring manager opens an approved requisition and defines the role outcomes and required skills.
2. Recruiting confirms the job description, interview plan, and accessible accommodations process before interviews begin.
3. Interviewers use the same role-related evaluation areas for every candidate and record evidence from the interview rather than personal impressions.
4. The hiring team compares evidence against the approved criteria and documents a clear decision rationale.
5. Recruiting completes references and any required checks with the candidate's consent, then coordinates the written offer and onboarding handoff.

Interview standards
Ask questions connected to the role's responsibilities. Give candidates a consistent opportunity to describe relevant experience. Do not ask about protected personal characteristics or collect unrelated sensitive information. Keep interview notes factual, respectful, and limited to information needed for the hiring decision.

Confidentiality
Candidate applications, interview notes, references, and offer details are restricted HR records. Share them only with staff assigned to the hiring process. Store records in approved recruiting systems and follow the retention schedule. Send correction or deletion requests to HR rather than copying candidate details into chat.
