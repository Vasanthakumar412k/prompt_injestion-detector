ACME Corporation — Remote Work Security Standard

Remote work must follow the employee's team arrangements and manager approval. Use a private, trusted network protected by a current router password. Avoid conducting company work over public Wi-Fi unless using the company-approved secure connection.

Keep company devices updated and protected with full-disk encryption and automatic screen locking. Store company files only in approved systems. Do not copy restricted information to personal email, personal cloud storage, or removable media without explicit authorization.

Position screens to reduce viewing by others, use a headset for confidential calls, and lock the device when stepping away. Report a lost device, suspected account compromise, or accidental disclosure promptly through the Security incident channel.

Managers should agree on availability, collaboration hours, and outcomes with remote team members. Remote work does not change the employee's responsibility to follow the company's data-handling and acceptable-use policies.
